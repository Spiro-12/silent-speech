# Security

Found a way to make the Mac app do something it shouldn't (run an action without being Armed or paired, reach it
from another website, read your text contact or your calendar)? Please report it privately:

- Go to the **Security** tab of this repo and click **Report a vulnerability**.
- Please don't open a public issue for it.

We aim to reply within a week. How the app protects you today: [docs/safety-privacy.md](docs/safety-privacy.md).
